## Industry-Specific Questions

### How do you ensure OMMA compliance for dispensaries?
We stay current with all **Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority** physical security requirements. Our guards are trained to manage high-traffic retail environments while maintaining the strict perimeter and access control logs required to protect your license.

### Do you provide equipment theft prevention for construction?
Yes. Construction sites in hubs like **Catoosa and Inola** are prime targets for material theft. We offer randomized vehicle patrols and 24/7 on-site guarding to protect your heavy machinery and raw materials after-hours.
