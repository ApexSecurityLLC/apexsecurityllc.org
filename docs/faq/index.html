Yes. All Apex Security LLC personnel are fully licensed through CLEET (Council on Law Enforcement Education and Training). We strictly adhere to all state mandates for both armed and unarmed security operations.

We are locally owned and law-enforcement-led. Unlike national “big box” security companies, our owner, Ryan, is directly involved in every contract. We hire guards who understand the specific needs of Pryor, Tulsa, and Northeast Oklahoma.

Can I hire guards for a one-time event?

Absolutely. We provide elite security for weddings, corporate galas, and community festivals. Whether you need a single night of coverage or a multi-year industrial contract, we scale to fit your needs.

Industry-Specific Questions

How do you ensure OMMA compliance for dispensaries?

We stay current with all Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority physical security requirements. Our guards are trained to manage high-traffic retail environments while maintaining the strict perimeter and access control logs required to protect your license.

Do you provide equipment theft prevention for construction?

Yes. Construction sites in hubs like Catoosa and Inola are prime targets for material theft. We offer randomized vehicle patrols and 24/7 on-site guarding to protect your heavy machinery and raw materials after-hours.
